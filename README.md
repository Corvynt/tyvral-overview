# Tyvral | Threat Intelligence for Connected Operations

**A Corvynt product initiative for security teams working across enterprise IT and operational technology.**

Tyvral is intended to help organizations make sense of cyber threat information in the context of the systems and operations they protect. Our initial areas of interest include advanced manufacturing, healthcare, energy and other critical infrastructure environments.

## The problem

Security teams often need to evaluate advisories, indicators and threat context across multiple sources while accounting for the operational constraints of industrial and healthcare environments. Corvynt is exploring how to help analysts organize that information and prioritize a human-reviewed response.

## Product direction

- **Threat context:** organize indicators, relevant advisories and sector context for analyst review.
- **IT and OT perspective:** connect intelligence to the operational environment and the assets a customer chooses to identify.
- **Analyst workflows:** support investigation, triage and concise briefings for technical and business audiences.
- **AI-assisted research:** explore agentic workflows that cite their sources and keep people in control of decisions.

These are design objectives, not claims that the features are available today. In particular, no autonomous blocking or changes to operational systems are implied. Corvynt would scope any future integration and response actions with the customer.

## Current stage

Tyvral is a product prototype. An existing private starter codebase includes early work on indicator management and sector briefing generation; authentication, customer isolation and application flows require further verification. Automated intelligence feeds, AI-agent workflows, customer integrations and subscription billing remain future work. The product is not yet presented as a generally available SaaS offering.
